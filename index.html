My postpartum roadmap

Fill this in before baby arrives (or in the first days), and come back to it when you feel lost. This isn't a contract: cross things out, change it, start over.

Everyone's are different. Naming them is already taking back control. Examples: I cry for no clear reason · I can't sleep even when the baby sleeps · everything irritates me · I don't want to see anyone

The ones already in your postpartum care plan: copy the 2-3 you'll turn to first here.

2 or 3 small things that keep you afloat. Not goals, lifebuoys. Examples: one shower a day · one hot meal · 10 minutes outside

3 steps, in order, decided with a clear head. E.g.: 1. I pause (I put the baby down somewhere safe and step out for 5 minutes) · 2. I call… · 3. If it doesn't lift (I call my midwife / my doctor)

Write it the way you'd talk to a friend. E.g.: "You're learning, and it's normal that it's hard."

This plan can change. Cross it out as much as you like: it's a compass, not a contract.
